Automatic action policy

The lead plans, an independent agent reviews the plan, milestones build, get reviewed and integrate, then it plans again — no approvals from you. When the lead thinks the goal is met, every available agent must independently agree; a single dissent records what is missing and the run continues. It stops by itself when the call budget or the planning round cap runs out, when the lead needs an answer, or when the agents agree it is done.
